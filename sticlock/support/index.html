Common questions

How do I change the clock color?
Tap anywhere on the clock.

What does Premium include?
Calm, endlessly changing music that fades out as your timer ends, and the hourglass effect that fills the digits with a new color. The practice timer itself is free. Premium is a one-time purchase.

I bought Premium on another phone. How do I get it back?
Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account and open SpiraClock — Premium is restored automatically.

I don't hear the music.
Turn up the media volume, and check that the speaker button in the corner shows 🔊, not 🔇.
