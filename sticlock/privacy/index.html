StiClock Privacy Policy

SpiraApps · Support

Last updated: October 31, 2026

StiClock is a bedside clock app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. StiClock does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings (clock color, sleep timer length, whether music is on, and whether premium is unlocked) on your device only. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The premium unlock is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether premium has been purchased on your account, so it can be restored on your devices. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Music

The music is generated on your device. No audio is downloaded, recorded or sent anywhere, and the app does not use the microphone.

Children

StiClock is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
