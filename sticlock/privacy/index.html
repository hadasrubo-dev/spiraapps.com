SpiraClock Privacy Policy

SpiraApps · Support

Last updated: November 14, 2026

SpiraClock is a practice timer, clock and wake-up alarm app for Android and iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. SpiraClock does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your settings on your device only: clock color, timer length, music on/off and style, your alarms, the alarm settings, the first name you type for the morning greeting, when your free month of Pro began, and whether Pro is unlocked. Your phone's own backup may copy them to a new phone. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

If you pick your own song for the alarm, the app keeps permission to read that one file so it can play it. It never copies, uploads or shares the file.

Purchases

Pro is free for the first month. After that, the Pro unlock is sold through Google Play (on iPhone, through the App Store, where Apple handles payment the same way). Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether premium has been purchased on your account, so it can be restored on your devices. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Music

The music is generated on your device. No audio is downloaded or sent anywhere.

Microphone and speech

The microphone is used only if you turn on voice commands for the alarm, and the app asks for permission at that moment. While an alarm is ringing or asking its wake-up question, the app listens for commands such as "snooze" or "I'm up" and for your answer. Nothing is recorded or saved, and the microphone is off at all other times.

Speech is recognized on your phone, so what you say does not leave it. Only if your phone has no offline English recognition, and you turn on the "Online speech" switch (off unless you turn it on), what you say while the alarm rings is sent to Google's speech service (on iPhone, Apple's) to be turned into text, under that company's privacy policy. We never receive it.

The spoken greeting and questions use your phone's built-in text-to-speech.

Children

SpiraClock is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
