SwingSlam Privacy Policy

SwingSlam · Support · Play safely

Last updated: November 8, 2026

SwingSlam is a motion game for iPhone with Apple Watch, and for Android with Wear OS. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking across apps or sites, and nothing is sold.

Motion

The watch or phone reads its own motion sensors to detect your swings. The sensor readings are processed on the device and summarised into a swing (its direction and strength); only that reaches the game. The last seconds of raw motion are kept in memory on the device for the Report described below, and are discarded otherwise.

Health (iPhone and Apple Watch)

SwingSlam starts a workout session on the watch so it can keep sending your swings while the watch screen is off. By default nothing is written to Apple Health. If you turn on Save to Apple Health, a play session is saved to Health as one workout with each game inside it (its kind, time, active energy and heart rate), and the app reads the heart rate and energy the watch measured during the game to show them to you afterwards and in its statistics. Health data stays on your devices and in your Apple Health; it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is never used for advertising. You can turn the setting off at any time in Watch setup, and remove the workouts in the Health app.

Anonymous usage data

To know which games are played and whether the app works, the app sends to our server, at launch and after a finished game: a random identifier the app made up and keeps (not tied to your name, Apple ID, Google account or hardware), the app and system version, the game played, its duration, score and level, the playing hand, whether the watch or the finger played, and counts and average speeds of swings. If the app crashes, it sends the crash's reason and stack. No names, no location, no contacts, no health data, no motion recordings. The server runs on Render in Frankfurt (EU).

Two players and a second screen

Playing with another phone, or showing the game on a TV or tablet, goes through a short-lived room on our server or directly over your Wi‑Fi. What passes through is the game's moves and scores and the nickname you typed (it is shown to the other player). Rooms are kept in memory only and are gone half an hour after the players leave. To find a phone close by, the app uses local networking (Bonjour) and, on request, the camera to scan the other phone's game code; the camera is used only while the scanner is open and no picture is kept.

Pictures (Hammer)

You can choose up to three of your own pictures for the Hammer game. Only the pictures you pick reach the app; they are stored on the device and never leave it.

Reports you send

Menu → Report writes a diagnostic file with the app's log, settings and the last 90 seconds of motion from the watch, and opens the share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you share it yourself (for example to support@swingslam.com). We use what you send only to fix the problem you reported.

Purchases

SwingSlam Full is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the store whether the purchase was made on your account, so it can be restored on your devices.

Data stored on your device

Settings, best scores, swing statistics and unlocked content are stored on the device, and removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

SwingSlam is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
