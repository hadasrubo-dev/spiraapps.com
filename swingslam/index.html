SwingSlam

SpiraApps · Play safely · Support · Privacy policy

Swing to play. SwingSlam turns your Apple Watch, Wear OS watch or the phone in your hand into a sword, a racket, a bat, boxing gloves, a golf club, a steering wheel. The game shows on the phone, or on a TV or tablet across the room.

The games

Slash and Blade (sword), Tennis and Ping pong, Boxing and Hammer, Golf and Mini golf, Hoops and Hockey, Race and Space. Play alone, or two players each with their own phone, in the same room or far apart.

How it reads your swing

The watch or phone measures how fast the arm turns and in which direction during a single swing, a few hundred milliseconds long, and sends the direction and strength to the game. Nothing tracks where you are in the room.

Health

With Save to Apple Health turned on (off by default), a play session with the watch is saved as one workout, each game inside it with its kind, time, energy and heart rate. These are the watch's estimates. SwingSlam is not a medical device.
