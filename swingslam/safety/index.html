Play safely

SwingSlam · Support · Privacy policy

SwingSlam is played by swinging the phone or the watch. The app shows this notice before the first game; here it is to read again.

Hold on tight — a swung phone can fly

Swinging the phone like a bat, racket or sword pulls it out of the hand: grip it firmly with your whole hand, use a case with a grip or a wrist strap, and dry sweaty hands. Never let go at the end of a swing. A phone that flies can break, break the TV or a window, or hurt someone. Wear the watch with its band snug.

Clear the space

Keep at least an arm's length and a step all round free of people, pets, furniture, lamps and ceiling fans, and stay well back from the TV. Mind the floor: no rugs to slip on, nothing to trip over.

Swing at the air only

Never hit anything or anyone. Holding the phone, never punch a bag; with the watch, a heavy bag can damage it.

Know your body

Warm up, start easy, take breaks and drink water. Stop at once if you feel pain, dizziness or short of breath. Ask a doctor before playing if you have a heart, joint or back condition, an injury, or are pregnant.

Children

Children play with an adult watching.

Flashing light

The games flash and move fast. If you or anyone in your family has had seizures or epilepsy, ask a doctor before playing; stop at once on any dizziness, twitching or blurred vision.

Not a medical device

Speeds, power, energy and heart rate are the game's and the watch's estimates — fun to compare, not measurements to train by or to judge your health by. A game saved to Health is an estimate too.

At your own risk

You play SwingSlam at your own risk. Its makers aren't responsible for injury, or for damage to the phone, the watch or anything around you.
