Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: support@swingslam.com. For a problem, please attach the app's Report (menu → Report → share), and mention your phone and watch models.

Common questions

The watch doesn't answer.
Start a game from the phone; it opens the watch app itself. If the status still says the watch has an older build, update SwingSlam on the watch in the Watch app on the phone.

My swings are read the wrong way round.
Check the playing hand on the menu (tap the hand badge), then run How to play: its four-slash check fixes a mirrored reading on the spot.

Nothing is saved to Health.
Save to Apple Health is off by default. Turn it on in Watch setup; the first saved game asks Health for permission to read heart rate and active energy.

How do I show the game on a TV?
Mirror the phone's screen to the TV (Control Center → Screen Mirroring), or run SwingSlam on a tablet or another phone and choose "Be the screen", then "Show on another screen" on the player's phone.

Can two of us play?
Yes: each player needs their own phone (and watch or the phone in hand). One invites with a code or QR, the other joins; in the same room the phones also find each other.

Is it safe to swing the phone?
Hold it with the whole hand, use a case with a grip or a wrist strap, and read Play safely.
